About Us

Roswell Robo Squad Robotics is a community program that helps students learn about robotics, engineering, coding, and science.

Robotics and STEM

Learning by Building

Students learn how robots work by designing, building, programming, and testing their creations. These activities help develop problem-solving skills.

Our Activities

Students can participate in activities such as:

  • Building and programming robots
  • Learning computer coding
  • Exploring engineering and 3D design
  • Working together on team projects
  • Preparing for robotics competitions

Our Goals

Preparing for the Future

Our goal is to help students develop creativity, teamwork, leadership, and technical skills for school, college, and future careers.
